What does it do?
================

With clubdirectory you can manage, list and show all clubs of your town.
Further we have added a form based on EXT:form for your FE users to
register their new club or edit them. The addresses of a club are geocoded
with maps2 and the position can be corrected on a map. The description can
be edited with a rich text editor.
